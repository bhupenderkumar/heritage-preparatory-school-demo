Why Families Choose Us

Heritage Preparatory School — The Difference

Discover why families across Atlanta trust us with their children's education.

👩‍🏫

Expert Faculty

Our faculty are passionate educators with a commitment to helping each student succeed. They are dedicated to creating a supportive and inclusive learning environment.

📚

Small Class Sizes

Our small class sizes allow for personalized attention and instruction, ensuring each student receives the support they need to succeed.

🏠

Safe Campus

Our campus is a safe and welcoming environment where students can feel comfortable and supported. We take the safety of our students very seriously and work hard to create a secure and nurturing community.

📊

Curriculum

Our curriculum is designed to be engaging, challenging, and relevant to the real world. We emphasize critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills to prepare students for success in the 21st century.

🌈

Whole-Child Education

We believe in educating the whole child - academically, emotionally, and socially. Our programs and activities are designed to help students develop essential life skills, such as empathy, self-awareness, and self-regulation.

🌟

Community

We're more than just a school - we're a community. We foster a sense of belonging and connection among our students, families, and faculty, creating a supportive and inclusive environment that welcomes everyone.
