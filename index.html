Why Families Choose Us

Heritage Preparatory School — The Difference

Discover why families across Atlanta trust us with their children's education.

👩‍🏫

Expert Faculty

Our experienced faculty are dedicated to helping our students reach their full potential. With a low student-to-teacher ratio, our students receive the individualized attention they need to succeed.

👥

Small Classes

Our small class sizes allow us to tailor our approach to meet the unique needs of each student. This ensures that our students receive the personalized attention they need to thrive.

🏫

Safe Campus

Our safe and secure campus provides a supportive and inclusive environment for our students to learn and grow. We take pride in creating a sense of community and belonging for all our students.

📚

Curriculum

Our rigorous curriculum is designed to challenge our students and help them reach their full potential. From core subjects to electives, our students have access to a wide range of courses and activities.

🌱

Whole-Child Education

At Heritage Preparatory School, we believe that education should be a holistic experience that encompasses not just academics, but also social, emotional, and physical development. Our programs and activities are designed to help our students become well-rounded and confident individuals.

🤝

Community

Our tight-knit community is built on a foundation of mutual respect, trust, and support. We believe that by working together, we can create a positive and inclusive environment that helps our students thrive.

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum that challenges, inspires, and prepares every student.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children who are 3-4 years old. Our experienced teachers use a play-based approach to help children develop their social, emotional, and cognitive skills.

📖

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students from Kindergarten to 4th grade. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to engage students and help them reach their full potential.

🔬

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper programs serve students from 5th to 8th grade. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to engage students and help them reach their full potential, while also preparing them for high school and beyond.
