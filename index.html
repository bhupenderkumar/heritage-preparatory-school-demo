Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum that challenges, inspires, and prepares every student.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-4, focusing on play-based learning, socialization, and early literacy skills.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students from Kindergarten to 5th Grade, emphasizing a strong foundation in core subjects, including reading, writing, mathematics, and science.

📚

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper school programs cater to students from 6th to 8th Grade, focusing on advanced academic studies, character development, and leadership skills.
