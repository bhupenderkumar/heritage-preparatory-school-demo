Our Mission & History

Heritage Preparatory School was founded in 2005 by a group of dedicated educators who sought to create a school that would provide a high-quality education in a supportive and inclusive environment. Since its inception, the school has grown to serve over 200 students from Pre-Kindergarten to 8th grade.

Our teaching approach is centered around the idea that every child learns differently. Our experienced faculty use a variety of teaching methods to engage students and help them reach their full potential. We also place a strong emphasis on character development and social responsibility, recognizing that these are essential skills for success in all areas of life.

At Heritage Preparatory School, we believe that extracurricular activities and character development are just as important as academics. We offer a range of programs and activities that help students develop their interests and talents, from arts and music to sports and community service.
